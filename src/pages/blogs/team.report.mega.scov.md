In April, *Pokemon Champions* debuted with the **lowest power-level format** we've seen since the launch of *Scarlet & Violet* four years ago. As such, I was strongly reinvigorated to play more. I love low power formats; they typically afford a lot more breathing room when compared to formats which allow more powerful Legendaries, where a single mistake can leave you on the receiving end of multiple one-hit-knock-outs. As we'd been in on-again-off-again Legendary formats for the last two years of *Scarlet & Violet*, my motivation to play waned and **I turned my focus towards my professorial duties**, organizing events for other players…

But a new game meant a clean slate, and **I was ready to jump back on to the battlefield** once more.

## REVELATION: THE FUNDAMENTAL TRUTHS
Anyone who is at least peripherally familiar with me probably knows that **my favorite Pokémon is Torkoal**, the tortoise that is also a kiln. I am fortunate in that my favorite Pokémon also has a strong competitive niche. Starting in *Sun & Moon*, Torkoal was **granted the ability Drought**, which sets up the **Harsh Sunlight** condition upon entering battle, increasing the power of Fire-type damage by 50% and reducing the power of Water-type damage by 50%. It is also one of the lucky few to have **access to Eruption**, one of the strongest Fire-type moves in the game, which is further strengthened by the sun. Lastly, Torkoal also boasts an **impressively low speed stat**, which would be detrimental if not for the existence of the **Trick Room** condition, which a partner pokemon can set up to invert the speed order of the battle for several turns.

As a result of wanting to use this funky little reptile as much as possible, **I have become a Trick Room gamer at heart**. I understand them on a spiritual level. Try as I may explore alternate strategies, I cannot deny who I am. I live inside the twisted dimensions. They are my home.

So, let's get to it, right? We've built a dozen Trick Room team compositions around Torkoal before. Let's get the gang back together and hit the ladder!

## DEVASTATION: THE FRIENDS WE LOST
Tragically, many of Torkoal’s best friends are tragically absent from the starting roster of Champions. 

### GONE: Liligant

Long-time life-partner Liligant, who doubled her speed with **Chlorophyll** under Torkoal’s sun, is still on vacation in Hisui. During the *Scarlet & Violet* era, the turn-1 lead of Liligant and Torkoal was one of the most volatile (and in my opinion, fun) mix-ups in the game; Liligant’s aforementioned speed increase under sun allowed her to **land a Sleep Powder before anyone else** had a chance to move, or, alternatively, **bequeath her bountiful speed to Torkoal** by targeting it with **After You**, invoking an immediate and incendiary Eruption. Hell, maybe she just hits you with her own **Close Combat** or **Leaf Blade**. Having Grass-type coverage was very nice, as it immediately menaced the Water-types which Torkoal struggled with.

The plethora of options forced opponents to commit to a difficult read. Do they switch in weather disruption and risk a Sleep Powder still landing in that vulnerable slot? Do they go for a Fake Out into one of the two, and possibly risk letting the other get an attack off? What if our Liligant terastalizes into the Ghost type to dodge it, or what if we switch our Indeedee in to block the move entirely?

### GONE: Indeedee

Speaking of Indeedee, she's also missing. An absolute pillar of Trick Room compositions, her access to **Follow Me, which redirects all single-target moves into herself**, and **her ability to set Psychic Terrain**, which both blocks priority and drastically improves the power of the attack Expanding Force, made her a best-in-slot support. She could even set Trick Room herself, or use Helping Hand to further empower her partner.

Historically, that partner has always been Hatterene on teams which I have built. The synergy between these two ladies is tremendous: Indeedee’s ability to block Fake Out and provide redirection, combined with Hatterene’s ability **Magic Bounce, which blocks disruptive status moves like Taunt or Spore**, meant that Trick Room was essentially guaranteed to go up. And once it was up, the duo was free to **unleash Helping-Hand-and-Psychic-Terrain-Boosted Expanding Forces**.

### HERE, BUT NOT VERY USEFUL: Hatterene

While Hatterene is a very appealing pick for Trick Room compositions, due to being able to both set up Trick Room and then sweep under it without requiring a pivot, the loss of her wife completely shutters her effectiveness. While Hatterene is enormously strong offensively, she is rather frail defensively, so without a partner who can both block Fake Out and redirect attacks, she is susceptible to the double-up taking her out immediately. **The lack of automatic Psychic Terrain to turn Expanding Force into an overtuned spread attack is also detrimental.**

So what do we do? How do we get Trick Room up and then actually capitalize on it in a way that allows Torkoal to thrive? What tools do we even have left?
